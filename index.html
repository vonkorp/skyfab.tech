Procédé

Three unfair advantages

01 —

Monocoque moulding

One-shot PU RIM rigid-skin moulding integrates structure, ventilation channels, and carbon interface zones directly. No secondary machining. No assembly joints to fail in the field.

02 —

Horizontal scaling

FDM PETG tooling at €800–2,000 per mould vs €5–20k for aluminium. Each additional mould adds direct capacity. Closed-loop PETG recycling targets 50–70% material substitution at scale.

03 —

Design agility

New mould operational in 2–5 days. Aligned with the 2–3 month obsolescence cycle observed on active theatres. The only airframe process that matches battlefield tempo.
